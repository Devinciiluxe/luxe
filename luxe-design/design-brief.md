# Design brief — LUXEdesign.online

- Brand: LUXEdesign — cinematic FPV drone flythroughs of exceptional hommes. Dark-luxury identity.
- Journey shape: custom — ONE client-supplied 12s 4K flythrough, sliced into five
  overlapping legs mapped to the flight's real beats (arrival → interior → pool →
  panorama ×2). Legs share an overlapping time window so scrub seams sit inside
  identical footage and crossfade invisibly. Posters are exact first frames of
  each encoded leg. No AI film generated — the client's own footage is the film.
- Journey (chapters): 01 The Arrival (brand promise) → 02 The Experience (what
  we do) → 03 Signature Flight $800 → 04 Estate Flight $1,400 → 05 Estate Grand
  Tour $2,000. After the journey: packages detail, process, booking, footer.
- World grammar: golden-hour modern villa, warm amber light, stone/glass/wood,
  ocean horizon. All legs from the same take, so grammar is identical by
  construction.
- Mobile framing: mobile encodes at 720p cap; copy pinned bottom; focal action
  is centered by the pilot's path.
- Palette (sampled from frames): champagne gold #D8B36A accent, warm black
  #0B0A08 canvas, warm ivory ink #F4EEE2, sand #C4B8A4 muted. Bright footage ⇒
  strengthened dark scrims behind every copy block.
- Type: Cormorant Garamond (display, italic accents) + Inter (UI labels,
  letterspaced uppercase kickers).
- Delivery budget: desktop ≈ 34 MiB over five lazy-loaded legs (4K masters,
  GOA 8, CRF 23) — accepted slightly above the 32 MiB guidance because the
  client's 4K footage is the product; mobile ≈ 8.8 MiB. Posters are 1920w JPG.
- Booking: mailto bookings@luxedesign.online (subject per tier), phone-free.
